# Audit of the PR Digital Twin against its purpose (Phase 1)

Date: 2026-10-05. Scope: the repository as of commit `dce07cf` (main). **No code or data was changed for this audit.** Numbers are measured from the files unless a line says "estimate".

Purpose used as the yardstick: a general, island-wide model of Puerto Rico that brings as much public information as possible onto one geography and one timeline, so any layer from any domain can be combined, compared and correlated with any other. The domain set is open and growing.

## 0. Summary

1. **Framing.** The data and most features are already general, but the identity and the defaults are not. The default map view is the tourism-places layer, the first section of the Layers tab is "Tourism supply", the municipality profile and the rank order lead with tourism, and the 3D page, README and SHARE.md still describe a tourism-and-hazards tool. 23 places in the code and documents need new wording or a new default, 9 of them identity or default problems (section 1).
2. **Domain coverage.** Of the six starting domains, **Housing** and **Economy** are strong, **Infrastructure** (strong for transit and roads only) and **Environment** (strong layers, narrow exposure indicators) are partial, and **Education** and **Health** are essentially empty (one education proxy; no health variable). 91 of the 594 documented items (population and households 12, public money and recovery 11, land and boundaries 68) fit no starting domain, plus 176 items that document measures, methods and map tools (section 2).
3. **Cross-analysis.** Only **108 datasets** (the Economy tab's stack, the workbench and the chat) can be combined and correlated. Everything computed in `municipalities.json` outside housing (tourism, flood, transit coverage, land use), all barrio statistics, and every polygon or point layer are outside that engine. Three other choropleth systems exist beside it (section 3).
4. **Architecture.** Four pages share data files but almost no code: plain scripts and globals, no modules, no tests. The same functions exist two to four times (color ramps, CSV export, sparklines, nearest-year logic, year bars). At least **14 lists hard-code datasets, groups or domains** and would have to move into a registry (section 4).
5. **Geography.** Two incompatible keys are in use (5-digit GEOID in the series files, accented municipality names everywhere else), no crosswalk file exists, and nothing below the barrio exists: no census tracts, block groups or block IDs (section 5).
6. **Hosting.** 65.0 MB raw, 15.6 MB compressed, 6.5% of the Pages limit. Storage is not the constraint. **First-load weight is:** the map downloads 17 MB (3.0 MB compressed) before it is usable, 96% of it the tourism places file, which is the wrong default for a general twin (section 6).
7. **Language.** The interface is English only. Only the chat and part of SHARE.md are bilingual. "Spanish first" is not met anywhere in the interface (section 7).

**Documented data that fits no starting domain (91 of the 594 dictionary items, plus 176 items about measures, methods and tools):**
- **Population and households** (7 series: population 2010-2025, age 65+, median age, households, household size, population change) and the denominators every other domain needs. The strongest candidate for a new domain.
- **Public money and recovery** (federal awards in five groups, FEMA Public Assistance, FEMA individual assistance: 9 series). Fits Economy loosely and a future governance or public-finance domain better.
- **Base geography** (municipio, barrio, region boundaries; 78 + 901 + 7) and the place-name index: foundations, not a domain.
- **Map tools and methods** (layers, measures, methods, the build and registry files): documentation, not data.

**Registry that already exists:** `dictionary_src/domains.json` holds area summaries and group descriptions keyed by English area name. It has no id, no Spanish name, no icon, no color token, no order, no sub-domains, and no dataset declares more than one area. `site/data/wanted.json` groups candidates by a different set of eight "themes" (Economy, Tourism, Hazards and resilience, Infrastructure and services, Health and education, Mobility and safety, Land and buildings, Governance and recovery). There are therefore three unrelated domain vocabularies today.

## 3. Cross-analysis capability

**Combine, compare and correlate today (the Economy tab, workbench and chat):** 108 datasets (57 economy, 46 housing, 5 tourism-supply counts), at municipio level, for any year, up to two shaded layers (bivariate) plus symbol layers, with normalization, inflation adjustment, fixed classes, a scatter with correlation, trend and a sortable table. The chat searches, ranks, correlates and trends only these 108.

**Outside that engine:**

| Data | Where it lives instead | What is missing |
|---|---|---|
| 47 non-housing fields of `municipalities.json`: tourism counts and densities, flood share, transit share, residents within 400 m, urban and protected land share | A separate "Shade by" menu (19 options, one variable, quintiles, its own legend) and a Rankings table | Cannot be stacked, correlated, trended or asked about. Single year. |
| Barrio statistics (901 barrios: places, lodging, flood, transit) | Popup only | No map, no table, no engine |
| Point layers: tourism places, transit stops, stations, EV, taxi | Clustered markers | Cannot be aggregated to municipio, hexagon or barrio to compare with area data |
| Polygon layers: flood, protected, land use | Drawn polygons | Exposure (population, housing, jobs inside a hazard) is never computed; only tourism places are tested |
| Transit coverage | Municipio fields and a gaps layer | Not in the stack |
| Hurricanes, sea level rise | 3D time bar | Not joined to anything |
| 3D columns | A hard-coded list of 11 metrics (`twin3d.js:24`) | Not the engine's datasets |
| Housing page maps | Its own maps, year bar and breaks | A parallel implementation of the same idea |

So there are **four** choropleth implementations (`app.js` CHORO, `housing.js`, `twin3d.js` METRIC, `economy.js`), three class-break methods (quintile, pooled quantile, three-way choice) and three legend styles.

## 4. Architecture

**Pages and what they share.** `index.html` (app.js, plan.js, roads.js, network.js, economy.js, chat.js), `3d.html` (twin3d.js, plan.js, roads.js), `housing.html` (housing.js) and `dictionary.html` (dictionary.js). The only shared code is `common.js` (8 helpers), `icons.js` (category icons), `plan.js` (search, planner, a `Data.json` fetch cache) and `roads.js`. Shared state is shared files: each page fetches the same JSON separately. There is no module system (globals and a `window.onMapReady` wrapper chain), no bundler and no automated tests; checks are manual browser runs plus `dictionary.py --check/--coverage/--links`.

**Duplicated logic (counts are occurrences in the page scripts):**
- Five-step blue color ramp: `app.js:73`, `housing.js:11`, `twin3d.js:23`, and `economy.js` `EC_RAMPS`.
- CSV writers: four (`ecSaveCsv`, `saveCsv` in dictionary.js, `downloadCsv` in app.js, one in housing.js).
- Sparklines: `housing.js` `spark` and `economy.js` `ecSpark`.
- Nearest-year resolution and pooled breaks: `housing.js` (`resolve`, `at`, `pooled`) and `economy.js` (`ecResolve`, `ecBreaks`).
- Year bar with play: `housing.js` and `economy.js`.
- Chart.js usage in two pages with separate setup; JSON loaders: `getJson` (app.js), `loadJson` (housing.js, twin3d.js), `Data.json` (plan.js), `fetch` (dictionary.js).
- Series metadata lives **three times**: Python catalogs (`economy_series.py` CATALOG, `housing_series.py` CATALOG), dictionary entries, and the JS dataset records built in `economy.js:29-50`.

**Hard-coded by domain or dataset (each one blocks an extensible registry):**

## 5. Geography

| Dataset | Join key | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `municipios.geojson` | `GEOID` ("72085") and `municipality` (name), `region` | Reference polygons |
| `economy_series.json`, `housing_series.json` | `geo.ids` (GEOID, last row "72") and `geo.names` | Both keys present |
| `municipalities.json` | Accented name only ("Bayamón") | No GEOID |
| `places.json`, `transport.json` | `municipality` name, `barrio` name (places) | No ids; text match |
| `barrios.geojson` | 10-digit county-subdivision GEOID, `barrio`, `municipality` name | 901 units |
| `population_blocks.json` | Longitude, latitude, residents (and one more number) | **No block GEOID** |
| `flood`, `protected`, `landuse` | Spatial only (`landuse` has a `municipio` name) | One water polygon spells "Guayanabo" |

- Names are matched by an accent-stripping `norm()` in at least six places; a misspelling or encoding change breaks a join silently.
- **No crosswalk exists** (island, region, municipio, barrio, tract, block group, block). Regions come only from `municipios.geojson`.
- **Nothing below the barrio:** no census tracts, block groups or block IDs. The blocks used for transit coverage cannot be joined to census tables because they carry no ID. The barrios are county subdivisions, so no population or housing figure is available for them.

## 6. Hosting and page weight

Measured with gzip level 6 on the files in `site/data/`. Load times below are **estimates** from file size, not measurements on a throttled device.

| Item | Raw | Compressed |
|---|---|---|
| All 25 data files | 65.0 MB (6.5% of the 1 GB Pages limit) | 15.6 MB |
| Largest: land use 17.7 MB, tourism places 16.7 MB, flood 8.0 MB, roads 6.4 MB | | 3.5, 2.9, 2.1, 3.5 MB |
| Dictionary | 2.2 MB | 0.5 MB |
| Scripts and styles (all pages) | | 164 KB |
| **Map page before it is usable** (meta, places, municipalities, boundaries) | **17.2 MB** | **3.0 MB** (places.json is 2.9 of it) |

- Estimate for the map's first load: 3.0 MB data plus about 0.2 MB scripts plus third-party libraries and tiles. On Slow 4G (about 1.6 Mbps) that is roughly 16-20 s of transfer; on Slow 3G (about 0.4 Mbps) over a minute. The 26,579 pins are also parsed and clustered before the map is interactive.
- Repository: `.git` is 30 MB; the local build cache is 2.8 GB (ignored). Largest single file 17.7 MB against the 100 MB per-file limit.
- Series density: 9.8 bytes per value (108,467 values in 1.07 MB). Estimate for growth: 20 variables x 12 years x about 1,000 tracts (to be confirmed from Census files) is about 2.4 MB, doubled with margins of error; 50 variables at about 2,600 block groups is about 30 MB. Storage still fits Pages; **loading it all at start does not.**
- What continued growth requires: per-domain lazy loading, PMTiles for large geometry, Parquet or similar for tabular series queried in the browser, and keeping a small JSON summary per dataset. The landing view should load only a registry file and small headline summaries.

## 7. Language, design and accessibility (from the rules in the brief)

- **Language:** `<html lang="en">`, every label in English. Spanish exists only in the chat replies and the SHARE.md paragraph. The dictionary, About, legends and tooltips are English only. Meeting "Spanish first" needs a string layer (UI and registry names in both languages) before the engine is generalized.
- **Color tokens:** the FPR tokens exist in `twin.css`, but the page scripts carry 321 literal hex colors (ramps, bivariate grid, chart line colors, icon and chip colors inline). The data palettes are not documented. The chart line palette pairs a red and a green; the bivariate grid and ramps have not been tested for color-blind safety. Treat both as unverified.
- **Consistency:** legends and popups differ by page (Layers legend, Economy legend, Housing legend, 3D legend). A new domain would not inherit one pattern.
- **Accessibility:** the last axe runs on the map, Economy tab, chat and dictionary reported no violations. Not verified: keyboard-only paths through the map and workbench, screen-reader use, a non-visual equivalent for every map (the workbench table covers shaded and symbol layers; point layers, 3D and the planner have none), contrast of data palettes against their backgrounds.

## 8. What I did not check

Real throttled-network timings; a keyboard-only or screen-reader pass; the tract and block-group counts for Puerto Rico (needed for the growth estimate); whether the Census and other sources named in `wanted.json` still serve Puerto Rico data (that is a Phase 2 check).

## 9. Decisions I need before Phase 2

1. **A new domain for population and households?** The audit shows seven series and every denominator outside the six starting domains. I recommend adding it as the first new domain, and treating public money and recovery as an Economy sub-domain until a governance domain has more data. Confirm or redirect.
2. **Spanish first:** it is a precondition for the registry (names in both languages). Should Phase 3 start with the string layer, or follow the registry?
3. **Landing view:** the plan will propose one headline indicator per domain on a neutral map. Should the tourism pins remain a toggle on that map, or move entirely into the visitor-economy module's view?
4. **Existing `dictionary_src/domains.json`:** it can be reshaped into the registry (ids, bilingual names, icon, color token, order, sub-domains). That changes `dictionary.py` and the dictionary page. Agree?
5. **Three vocabularies:** dictionary areas (10), `wanted.json` themes (8) and the new domains. I will propose one mapping. Any domain names you already want fixed?
