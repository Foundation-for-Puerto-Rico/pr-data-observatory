# Tourism places: private homes, sensitive places and contact details

Owner rule of 2026-10-18 (the project lead): private residences, and any listing whose name is a street address or house number, are counted but never drawn and never published by name or address. Sensitive places (domestic-violence or children's shelters, addiction treatment and similar) are treated the same way. Holiday rentals are counted, not drawn: no name, no point, even when no unit number names them, because a holiday rental is usually someone's home; hotels, guest houses, inns and B&Bs that operate as lodging businesses stay drawn (HANDOFF decision 48, decided the same day). Standing rule: never publish business contact details or any personal data.

Decision 47 (the project lead, 2026-10-18, applied the same day on branch `tourism-counts`): homes, housing and care services are not tourism, so they are not counted either. A listing withheld as `residential`, `residence` or `sensitive`, or as `address` in a category where homes are filed, is dropped before every count and is not in `places.json` at all (`places_privacy.not_tourism`). Holiday rentals, and address-named units filed as hotels, B&Bs, rentals, cabins or campgrounds or as businesses, are tourism supply: counted, not drawn.

This page says how `places.json` (Overture Maps places, `build.py`) follows that rule. Code: `places_privacy.py`. Reviewed list: `places_review.json`. Tests: `tests/test_places_privacy.py`.

## What is published

| Place | Fields in `places.json` |
|---|---|
| Drawn (31,536 of 32,541 in the 2026-10 snapshot) | Name, Overture id, point, category, type, flags and transit fields, as before. **No street address, website, telephone, email, social account, postcode or brand.** `has_website: true` says only that a website is listed (it keeps the Municipality tab's "with a website" count). A telephone number written into a name is removed (4 names). |
| Counted, not drawn (1,005: holiday rentals 971, address-named rented units 34) | `withheld` (the reason), municipality, barrio, category, type, Overture category, confidence cut down to the map filter's 0.05 step (0.57 is written 0.55), source, duplicates, flood, protected-area and land-use fields, `transit_m` coarsened to one value per class (below), `transit_400m`, `hub_400m`, `has_website`. **No name, Overture id, point, nearest stop or update date.** |
| Not tourism (2,577 in the 2026-10 snapshot: residential 2,301, residence 157, address in a home category 67, sensitive 52) | Not published and not counted (decision 47). |


The withheld rows come last (below), and `place_access.json` follows the same order. The Municipality tab, the "places shown" choropleths, `municipalities.json`, `barrios.geojson` and the access shares all include the withheld places that are counted. The map, the 3D view, search, the flood list, hexagons and the CSV download use only the places with a point (`placeDrawn` in `js/access.js`).

The nightly transit refresh (`access.py`) cannot measure a place without its point, so a withheld place keeps the access values of the last full build: its columns in `place_access.json`, its `transit_m` and `transit_400m`, and `hub_400m` (a rail station or ferry terminal within 400 m). A full build measures it again from the cached Overture point.

## How a place is withheld

Overture gives no ownership or use field, and Meta users file homes, condominiums, public housing and whole subdivisions under `lodging` and `historic_site`. So name patterns pick the listings and the Overture category says what a match means (`places_review.json`, `categories`, each list with its reason):

| Reason | Rule | 2026-10 snapshot (all / default basis) |
|---|---|---|
| `sensitive` | A care, shelter or treatment word (hogar de envejecientes or de niños, égida, albergue, casa protegida, rehabilitación, Teen Challenge, hospicio, senior living, institución hogar, casa hogar, hogar de restauración, hogar de crianza, group home, halfway house, orphanage...) in any category; in the lodging, rental and unit categories, any name that starts with "Hogar" (a licensed care home is often filed as "Hogar <name>, Inc." under a hotel or B&B category). | 45 / 33 |
| `address` | A street address, house, lot or unit number in the name ("Urb. ... Calle ... K7", "12 Calle ...", "Apt. 208") in any category; a home word followed by a unit or house number ("Casa Ejemplo 28", "Cluster 2 - 39B") in any category, unless the name also names a public venue, a lodging business or a food or drink business (outside the categories where homes are filed or rented, a one-digit number is a branch number and does not count); a postcode, "#27", a lot code or "Street 1571" only where homes and rented units are filed (businesses number their branches: "Pizza #2"). | 92 / 72 |
| `residential` | A housing word first ("Urb.", "Residencial", "Parcelas", "Housing", "Elderly") in any category; a building or estate word (condominio, apartments, towers, jardines, estancias, villas de...) where homes are filed; a `lodging` listing that names no accommodation (hotel, guest house, rental, resort, camping...); the `mobile_home_park` category. | 2,417 / 2,259 |

A home or estate word does not withhold a place when the name also names an accommodation business ("Villa ... Guest House", "Casa ... Vacation Rentals") or a public venue named first ("Parque de Pelota, Urb. ...", "Playa Villa ...", "Iglesia ... Parcelas ..."). In every other category (restaurants, bars, hotels, guest houses, museums, churches...) "Casa ...", "Villa ..." or "Hacienda ..." is a business name and the place stays.

`places_review.json` holds the exceptions: 49 listings drawn by review, 48 of them although their name or category matches: 13 filed as holiday rentals whose name says guest house or hotel (decision 48); 13 hotels, guest houses, hostels and aparthotels filed as hotels, B&Bs or hostels whose name is "Casa/Villa" and one word, names units or ends in "House", each with a booking-site or travel-guide presence as a business that rents rooms, named in `source` (reviews of 2026-10-18); a bar named after the road it stands on; and 20 (each with its name and reason: city-hall-owned or public museums and cultural centers consistent with `nrhp_review.json`, a public reserve, bars and cafés called "El Albergue" or "El Huérfano", a surf break, observation towers, a betting agency's branch number); a tour business filed under `lodging` (review of PR #62); and an office building filed as a historic site, which matches no pattern but is recorded with its source (follow-up of PR #62); and 40 withheld although no pattern matches, by Overture id only with a note (`withheld_notes`). A listing in neither list is decided by the patterns, so after a refresh an unreviewed match is withheld, not drawn.

## Groups found (2026-10 snapshot, before the change)

- **Categories.** The tourism taxonomy keeps no residential Overture category except `mobile_home_park` (1). The homes are in tourism categories: `lodging` (1,510 listings, of which only 86 name an accommodation business; the rest are subdivisions, condominiums, public housing estates, barrios, elderly homes and private homes) and `historic_site` (2,120, of which 1,091 are withheld: condominiums, apartment buildings, urbanizaciones, public housing estates, homes and elder-care buildings).
- **Home-like names.** "Casa ..." 860 names, "Residencia/Residencial/Residence" 187, "Hacienda" 319, House/Home/Hogar 449, in all categories; most are restaurants, guest houses or churches and stay.
- **Same houses as the National Register.** 17 listings lie within about 330 m of a National Register listing that `nrhp_review.json` counts as a private residence and carry its name (among them the haciendas the PR #58 review named), and one more 1.5 km away. All 18 are withheld now, 15 by the patterns and 3 by id (filed as a bar, a religious organization and an art museum); the art museum is borderline (below). Other places with the same names far away (a museum in Old San Juan, guest houses, restaurants) are different places and stay.
- **Sensitive services.** 32: elderly homes, égidas and senior housing (most), a hospice, a rehabilitation center and an addiction-recovery ministry, a protected house, a men's boarding house under a person's name, and a park whose name says drugs (borderline).
- **Contact fields.** Street address 28,810 places, website 9,683 (886 social-media pages, 175 map short links, 28 WhatsApp links, 11 of them with a telephone number), telephone-like text in 4 names and at least 6 addresses. No telephone, email or social field was published (removed 2026-10-01), and there is no contact-name field. All addresses and websites are now gone; the 4 names lost their telephone number.

## What the tourism counts include (decision 47, 2026-10-18)

Until 2026-10-18 the withheld places were in every tourism count: 3,118 of the 26,579 counted places (11.7%) on the default basis. Most were not tourism: homes, housing estates, condominiums and care services that Meta users file under `lodging` and `historic_site`. The project lead decided to drop those (HANDOFF decision 47), and `build.py` now leaves them out before every count (`places_privacy.not_tourism`).

**The rule.** Dropped: `residential` (subdivisions, condominiums, housing estates, barrios filed as lodging), `residence` (private homes and person-named listings) and `sensitive` (care homes, shelters, recovery homes), in any category. For `address` (a name that is a street address or a house, lot or unit number) the category decides: filed where homes are filed (`categories.ambiguous`: lodging, historic site, park, beach and similar) it is a numbered house, flat or lot and is dropped (67 listings, 53 on the default basis; one whose name also says holiday rental is recorded as `holiday_rental` and kept); filed as a hotel, B&B, holiday rental, cabin or campground (`categories.rental`) it is a numbered unit rented to visitors, and filed as a business (a restaurant, a bar) it is a business named by its address, so both stay counted (34 listings, 27 on the default basis). Kept: every `holiday_rental` (971; 715 on the default basis).

**Precedence (review of PR #62).** The reasons are tested in a fixed order and the first that applies is recorded, so `residential` (tested before `holiday_rental`) once took 141 listings whose name also says holiday rental, 114 of them on the default basis. A listing that matches `holiday_rental` is now recorded as `holiday_rental` (counted, not drawn) unless its name says housing clearly (`places_privacy.clear_housing`): a subdivision or estate word ("Urb.", "Residencial", "Estancias", "Villas de"...), a condominium or apartment building named as such ("Condominio ...", "Edificio ...", "Torre ..."), or a neighborhood, sector, barrio or town of the gazetteer that lies within 3 km of the place. A weaker signal gives way: a lodging listing that names no accommodation, "... Apartments", "... Studios", "... Condo", a home word. "Villa Ejemplo" is a subdivision only when the gazetteer maps a Villa Ejemplo nearby; "Villa de Ejemplo" alone is a house name. 104 of the 141 moved back into the counts (78 on the default basis); the other 37 are named condominiums and gazetteer subdivisions. "Lake House" and "River House" now name a holiday rental.

**Building words (review of PR #62).** "Edificio", "Edif." and "Building" name any building, so outside the `lodging` category they are no longer evidence of housing (a court building filed as a monument, office buildings filed as historic sites are drawn); "Court" still names a housing complex unless a public venue is named before it. National Register: the listings matched to a drawn National Register place are a former bank (its `historic_site` listing is drawn; a duplicate filed under `lodging` stays out) and a condominium in a listed apartment building (housing, stays out); the historic houses that stay out are not drawn National Register places (`nrhp_review.json` reviews them as residences or does not list them). The review file adds 3 listings withheld by id that the building rule would have drawn (a condominium and two numbered buildings) and, from a sample of 40 of the 497 rows dropped only because a `lodging` listing names no accommodation, 6 holiday rentals (by id) and 1 tour business drawn by review; the other 33 of the sample read as barrios, sectors, towns, subdivisions or homes, or are unclear, and stay out.

**Follow-up of PR #62 (2026-10-18).** Two groups the review of PR #62 left open were checked by web search and withheld by id when they are housing (`places_review.json`, `withheld_notes`; no names). Seven listings named "Edificio" (or "Edif.", "Edf.") and a person's name, filed as historic sites in town centers, were drawn under the building-word rule: one is a commercial building of medical and professional offices and stays drawn (`public`, with its source); four are subsidized or elderly housing in the Puerto Rico Housing Finance Authority's lists or in housing directories (`residential`); two could not be confirmed as public or commercial buildings and are withheld as `residence` (when in doubt, hidden). Of the 104 listings that the precedence rule moved from `residential` back to `holiday_rental`, 9 are named as a known subdivision, urbanización, community or public housing estate that the gazetteer does not map within 3 km but OpenStreetMap or Wikipedia's lists of sectors place at or near the listing (`residential`); a tenth has the name of a mapped subdivision but is rented whole to visitors under that name (booking-site listings), so it stays a holiday rental, now by id (review of PR #63); 4 lodging listings named as apartment buildings with no holiday-rental or booking-site presence are taken as long-term apartments (`residential`, borderline); and a home-services business filed as lodging, at a rural road address, is a service run from a home (`residence`), as the `home_business` categories are. 20 listings in all (17 on the default basis) left the counts; the count is 24,209 (24,226 before), and 6 fewer places are drawn.

**Result (2026-10 snapshot).** 2,577 listings left out (2,370 of them on the default basis: residential 2,172, residence 106, address 53, sensitive 39; 1,275 accommodations and 1,085 attractions, nearly all of them `lodging` and `historic_site`). The places file holds 32,541 rows (35,118 before); the default basis counts 24,209 places (26,579 before), of which 742 are counted but not drawn. The rebuilt figures: `municipalities.json` (all place counts, rates, flood, protected-area, website, transit, access and land-use fields), `barrios.geojson`, `place_access.json`, `meta.json` (counts, access, `transport.places_near_transit`, `landuse.places_on_protected_land`, new `counts.not_tourism_dropped`) and the catalog headlines. They were rebuilt with the build's own functions from the cache (no download), and the same run without the rule reproduces the published files of `main` exactly. The re-identification residual of the withheld rows mostly goes with them (below).

Two counts are measured from the points, so they leave the withheld places out, and both say so under the figure: the route planner's "tourism places within 400 m" and the study areas' "Tourism places". A withheld place has no point, and its municipio or barrio cannot say whether it is within 400 m of a route or inside a drawn area, so adding it from those would not be honest.

## What the withheld rows still say

A withheld row has no name, id or point. Under decision 49 (2026-10-18) its other values were made coarser wherever that changes no published figure:

- `transit_m` (any mapped stop): 400 (within 400 m), 700 (more than 400 and less than 800), 800 (exactly 800, cut) or 1000 (more than 800). The readers use `transit_400m` for 400 m, `< 800` (`access.js`) and `<= 800` (the nightly refresh), so each keeps its class.
- In `place_access.json`, `ts_m` and `tw_m`: 400, 800, 1000 (more than 800, up to the 1,000 m walk bound) or 99,999 (beyond it); a place walked in a straight line (`tw_straight`) keeps `tw_m` equal to `ts_m`, and a walk is never shorter than the straight line. `ts_calls` and `tw_calls`: 0, 1 (1 to 19) or 20 (20 or more). The weekend flags are kept.
- The update date is dropped and the confidence cut down to the slider's 0.05 step.
- The withheld rows come after all the drawn rows, sorted by their own published fields (`publish_order`), and `place_access.json` follows the same order. In Overture's order, which follows location, a withheld row sat next to drawn rows a median 0.45 km from it (70% within 1 km), so its position gave its place away.

When decision 49 was applied, every figure was unchanged: compared with `main` at the time, 225,484 counts (21 confidence steps, with and without worship, each municipio and Puerto Rico, by category, type, flood, protected area, website, transit, barrio and land use) and 63,741 access counts (`transit_m` < 800 and <= 800, `ts_m` and `tw_m` within 400 m, 800 m and the walk bound, frequent stops, departures, weekend service, detours) differ in none; `access.py` recomputes the published access fields without a change.

Uniqueness: before decision 49, 3,503 of the 3,509 withheld rows were unique on their published fields; after it, 3,086 of 3,588 (3,196 with the `place_access.json` values of the same row). Since decision 47 the homes, housing and care services are no longer in the file, so 1,005 withheld rows remain, and 939 of them are unique on their published fields (954 with their `place_access.json` values). What singles them out is the fields every count reads (municipio, category, type, confidence step, barrio, land use, flood). Removing that would change published counts (for example the barrio counts by category), so it is left: publishing the withheld places as counts by municipio and barrio instead of rows would be the next step, and is a decision for the project lead.

## Checks

- `python places_privacy.py --check`: exits 1 when a drawn place matches a pattern without a review, when a withheld place keeps its name, id, point, nearest stop or update date, has a confidence off the 0.05 step or a distance or departure count that is not coarsened, or comes before a drawn place, when a name holds a telephone or an email, or when a contact field is published. `pages.yml` runs it before every deploy; `check_refresh.py` runs the same check on the nightly refresh; `tests/test_places_privacy.py` runs it with the rule tests.
- `python places_privacy.py --report`: counts by reason and type, no names.
- After a refresh, `tests/test_places_privacy.py` lists review entries whose Overture id no longer exists: review them again.
- `tests/test_places_privacy.py` also fails when the name of a withheld place appears in any tracked text file (code, docs, tests, the review file, HANDOFF, the generated data). It reads the full rows from the Overture cache (`cache/overture_pr_places.geojson`, not in the repository), so it skips only when the cache is missing. A name that a drawn place or a public register in the repository also uses (a barrio, community, public housing estate, tax credit housing project or stop) is a public place name and is not counted. HUD's tax credit housing layer (`lihtc.geojson`) is such a register since 2026-11-05: its projects are housing that HUD names, as public housing is; a project whose name says it is a care home (`facilities.lihtc_care_name`: the `SENSITIVE` and `CARE_HOME_FIRST` words of this module) is counted, never drawn or named, and `tests/test_facilities.py` fails if the published layer names one. Analysis files under `docs/` that list names from a federal register write counts where a name may be a withheld place (`fac_check.json`, 2026-11-05). Write invented examples ("Casa Ejemplo 12", "Calle Ficticia 123", "Jane Doe's Beach House") and refer to a withheld place by its Overture id only.

## Adding a review

A place drawn that should not be: add its Overture id to `withheld` with a reason (`residence`, `residential`, `address`, `sensitive` or `holiday_rental`) and a line in `withheld_notes` (no name). A public place withheld by a pattern: add it to `public` with its name and the reason (it is a public place's name). Then run `python places_privacy.py` (it masks the published file without a rebuild and drops a row that is not tourism), `python dictionary.py`, `python catalog.py`, `python registry.py` and the checks. A row dropped as not tourism (`residence`, `residential`, `sensitive`, or `address` in a home category) changes the counts: `places_privacy.py --check` then fails until a full `python build.py` rebuilds `municipalities.json`, `barrios.geojson` and `meta.json`.
