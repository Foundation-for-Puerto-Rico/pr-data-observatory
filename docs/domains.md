# Domains of the PR Data Observatory

Generated by `python dictionary.py --domains` from `dictionary_src/domains.json`. Do not edit by hand: add or change a domain in the registry.

A domain is a field of information (housing, education and so on). The set is open and grows as data are added. A dataset belongs to one primary domain and may carry secondary ones. Rules and the placement guide: [adding-data.md](adding-data.md).

**Where visitors meet them (navigation plan, HANDOFF decision 81).** On the 2D map each top-level domain is a topic of the Explore tab: a card with its headline figure, and a page with its ready-made views, its datasets by area, its points, lines and areas, what is also in it, and FPR's analyses on it. The layer menu of the 2D map and the 3D view, and the dataset menus (Tools › Analysis, Rankings, the 3D columns), list their groups in the same order. A dataset's primary domain is its home topic; its other domains list it under "Also in this topic". The system domains are not topics.

## Descriptions

### Population and households / Población y hogares (`population`)

- EN: Who lives in Puerto Rico: how many people and households there are, their ages and how the numbers change.
- ES: Quién vive en Puerto Rico: cuántas personas y hogares hay, sus edades y cómo cambian las cifras.
- Icon: `groups`; accent token: `--fpr-green`; status: active
- Headline indicator: `housing.population`
- Example questions: "Which municipalities lost the most people?"; "How many people live in each municipality?"
- Ready-made views: Population and its change; Older and younger residents

### Economy / Economía (`economy`)

- EN: Jobs, businesses and income: where work is, what it pays, who is looking for it, poverty, the commute and what the visitor economy adds.
- ES: Empleos, negocios e ingresos: dónde está el trabajo, cuánto paga, quién lo busca, la pobreza, el viaje al trabajo y lo que aporta la economía del visitante.
- Icon: `work`; accent token: `--fpr-yellow`; status: active
- Headline indicator: `economy.qcew_jobs`
- Example questions: "Where are the jobs?"; "Which municipalities have the most poverty?"
- Ready-made views: Jobs and pay; Poverty and unemployment; Job centers and commuting

### Visitor economy / Economía del visitante (`economy.visitor-economy`)

- EN: Places and services that serve visitors: attractions, lodging, food and drink and visitor services, counted and mapped.
- ES: Lugares y servicios para visitantes: atracciones, alojamiento, comida y bebida y servicios al visitante, contados y mapeados.
- Icon: `luggage`; accent token: `--fpr-yellow`; status: active
- Headline indicator: `economy.tourism_places`
- Example questions: "Where is the most lodging?"; "How many attractions are there in each municipality?"
- Ready-made views: Tourism economy

### Public money / Dinero público (`public-money`)

- EN: Federal contracts, grants, loans and direct payments, and disaster recovery money, by place and year.
- ES: Contratos, subvenciones, préstamos y pagos directos federales, y fondos de recuperación por desastres, por lugar y año.
- Icon: `account_balance`; accent token: `--fpr-yellow`; status: active
- Example questions: "How much federal money did each municipality receive?"
- Ready-made views: Federal money per resident; FEMA disaster funding; Federal money spent by the municipio government

### Housing / Vivienda (`housing`)

- EN: Homes in Puerto Rico: how many there are, who lives in them, what they cost and how affordable they are.
- ES: Las viviendas de Puerto Rico: cuántas hay, quién vive en ellas, cuánto cuestan y qué tan asequibles son.
- Icon: `home_work`; accent token: `--fpr-blue`; status: active
- Headline indicator: `housing.median_gross_rent`
- Example questions: "Where is rent highest?"; "Which municipalities have the most vacant homes?"
- Ready-made views: Housing costs; Vacant homes; Public housing

### Health / Salud (`health`)

- EN: Health and care: hospitals and clinics, access to services, coverage and health conditions.
- ES: Salud y cuidado de la salud: hospitales y clínicas, acceso a servicios, cobertura y condiciones de salud.
- Icon: `health_and_safety`; accent token: `--fpr-red`; status: partial
- Headline indicator: `health.hc_sites`
- Example questions: "Which municipalities have no health center site?"; "Where is the shortage of primary care physicians greatest?"

- ES: Escuelas, colegios y universidades y lo que ha estudiado la gente: dónde están las escuelas, a cuántos estudiantes sirven y el nivel educativo de los residentes.
