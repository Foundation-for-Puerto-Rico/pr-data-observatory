One list for the whole observatory. This page is drawn from the Knowledge registry (knowledge_src/items.json in the project repository), the same list the area pages read. "Also in" names the other topics an item belongs to; "Data" names the published datasets it uses, each described in the data dictionary.
In review. An item marked "In review" is published, but its content may change while the review named on its card is open. Pages drafted with the help of artificial intelligence say so at the top.
