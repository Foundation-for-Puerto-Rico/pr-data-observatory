3D + Time

The immersive view of the PR Data Observatory: Puerto Rico with real terrain and building heights, and three clocks. Watch a day of transit, see sea level rise by year, and replay hurricane tracks since 1851.

Developed by Foundation for Puerto Rico as a tool for public use and benefit. It uses public data. Most sources have an open license, but some, such as the municipal transit feeds and the Junta de Planificación's maps, state none. The Data management page gives the license class of every file.

How to use it

  • Move around. Drag to pan, right-drag (or Ctrl-drag) to rotate and tilt, scroll to zoom. The buttons at the right do the same, and a help button lists the keyboard shortcuts.
  • Pick a clock. The time bar at the bottom switches between a day of transit, sea level by year and storms; the Time tab explains each. Press play to animate it.
  • Turn layers on and off on the Layers tab: flood zones, municipality columns, transit and more.
  • Plan a route. On the Route tab, name a start, an end and any stops (or choose them on the map). The camera can fly along it.
  • See it in 2D with the Map button for layers, economy data and the chat, and the Housing page for the 2023 State of Housing study.

Sources, licenses and how to cite

Every source behind this view, with its license, the credit line to use and how to cite it, is in the data dictionary. It also explains each layer and measure. Base maps and terrain come from OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap and Mapzen/AWS terrain tiles; hazards from FEMA and NOAA; transit from ATI and municipal feeds.

The Data management page lists every published data file with its size, records, fields and values, when it was first published and last updated, and its compliance review.

Moving around
Mouse
Drag to pan. Right-drag (or Ctrl-drag) to rotate and tilt. Scroll to zoom. Double-click to zoom in.
Touch
One finger pans. Two fingers zoom, rotate, and, dragged up or down together, tilt.
Keyboard
Click the map first. Arrows pan. Shift + arrows rotate and tilt. + and − zoom. N north up, T top-down or tilted, H whole island, Q / E rotate, V clean view, / search.
Buttons
The buttons at the right zoom, rotate, tilt, look straight down, return to the island and find your location. The compass resets north.
